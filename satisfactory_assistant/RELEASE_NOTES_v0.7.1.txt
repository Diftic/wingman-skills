Satisfactory Assistant v0.7.1

Player-facing machine and resource aliases for location maps.

- Oilfield, oil node, oil pump, and oil extractor requests now resolve to the
  save's Oil Extractor buildings instead of requiring the internal OilPump
  class name or the broader Crude Oil line name.
- Location matching now understands official production-building names,
  singular/plural forms, model suffixes, and the raw save classes. This covers
  miners, smelters, foundries, constructors, assemblers, manufacturers,
  refineries, packagers, blenders, particle accelerators, converters, quantum
  encoders, oil/water extractors, and resource-well equipment, with generator
  aliases included as well.
- Map pins use player-facing building labels instead of unresolved
  Persistent_Level resource references or internal Build_* names.

The standalone top-centre popup and its Wingman settings are unchanged from
v0.7.0.
