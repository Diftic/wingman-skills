Satisfactory Assistant v0.7.2

Focused, complete location maps and speech-routing recovery.

- Faster Whisper's observed "oilless extractor" transcription is normalized
  to Oil Extractor instead of falling back to broad oil-production matches.
- Explicit building-type queries now exclude production-line centroids and
  other oil-related buildings. The live test query returns all 17 Oil
  Extractors, not three Crude Oil lines plus two extractors.
- Spoken responses remain capped at five coordinates, while the map includes
  up to 100 matching buildings. Pins are numbered and the map reports total
  coverage.
- Location maps frame the matching buildings instead of the entire 1,185-
  machine factory. Nearby factory points remain as orientation context.
- If the model mistakenly sends a recognized building type through the
  context tool's focus field, the skill now runs the location path and opens
  the popup instead of claiming that no planning record exists.

The standalone top-centre popup and machine aliases remain as introduced in
v0.7.0 and v0.7.1.
